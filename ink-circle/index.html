07 DECEMBER

Ensō.

One circle in fresh snow.

An ensō can stay open or come full circle. Trace yours through the powder in one motion. Lift when the circle feels finished.

Draw with a mouse, finger, or pen. Move slowly for a fuller line. With a pen, vary your pressure.

Touch the snow to draw your own circle.

Your score

Keyboard: focus the snow and hold Space to trace a circle. Release to lift.
