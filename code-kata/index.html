Move reference

Read Frostbyte’s stance before you commit. Each valid line gives both fighters one turn. Chain up to three moves with a dot.

move(left) · move(right)
Step away or close the gap. Attacks reach within two tiles.
jump(forward) · jump(back)
Travel two tiles and clear a low sweep.
kick() · punch()
Aim at legs, mid, body or face. Low guard covers legs and mid. High guard covers body and face. Try punch(mid) or kick(face, hard). Kick defaults to legs, punch to body. Both default to fast.
sweep()
Sweep the legs with a fast low strike. Use sweep(legs, hard) for a heavier hit.
uppercut(face, hard)
A heavy high attack breaks a low guard.
block()
Brace against the next counterattack this turn.

The second argument is optional. Hard hits deal more damage, but lose power against an incoming jab. Repeated attacks in a chain do half damage.

Dojo secrets
hadooken()
Send a fireball through either guard from any distance.
shoryuken()
Close two tiles with a rising uppercut. Clears a sweep even if the strike falls short.
freezeframe()
Freeze Frostbyte for the rest of this turn, including his counter.
