    How to play

    Tap or drag two mittens under the lamp. Flip them to check the palms, or open a cuff to see inside. A pair needs a left and a right from the same owner. Return the pair with the button, or drag an inspected mitten onto it. Each pair earns a first-try point if neither mitten has been offered in a wrong pairing. Take as long as you like.
